GETTING STARTED

Set up in under 10 minutes.

No training required. If you can use a smartphone, you can run your coaching business on IronLog.

01

Create your account

Sign up with email. Immediately enable Trainer Mode in settings — no review or approval process.

02

Invite your first client

Your client downloads IronLog free and creates an account. You search their username and send an invite. They accept in one tap.

03

Build their program

Open their Split tab. Add exercises to each day using the exercise browser. Set sets, reps, and rest. Takes 5–10 minutes for a full week.

04

Send the plan

Tap "Send Proposal." Your client sees the new plan in their app and approves it. It goes live immediately after their confirmation.

05

Every time they train, the session logs in real time. Visit their History tab to see exactly what they did, down to individual sets.

06

Adjust & repeat

When they plateau or hit a new PR, update the plan. Push the change live in seconds. No PDFs, no emails, no confusion.

QUESTIONS

Common questions
from trainers.

Do my clients need to pay anything?
No — the IronLog athlete tier is completely free. Your clients download the app, create an account, and connect with you at zero cost. You're the only one who pays.
Can I see my client's workouts in real time?
You can see their full history after each session, including every set, weight, and reps. There's no live-stream during the workout, but the data is available the moment they finish.
What if my client already has their own plan?
Their existing plan stays until you send them a new one. When you do, it arrives as a proposal — they can review it before it replaces their current program. No surprises.
Can I manage gym clients and remote clients the same way?
Yes. IronLog works for in-person and remote coaching identically. Both client types log workouts, receive plans, and message you through the same interface.
What if a client doesn't have a smartphone?
IronLog is a mobile-first web app accessible from any browser. It works on desktop too — any device with a modern browser can run it, including older phones.
Is my client data private?
Yes. You can only see data for clients who have accepted your trainer invitation. Clients see their own data. No data is shared publicly or with third parties.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No long-term contract. Cancel from your account settings and you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.
