THE CORE THESIS

Free for athletes.
Paid for trainers.

The athlete side of the app is free forever — this drives adoption and makes every trainer's client roster sticky. Revenue comes from trainers who get real, measurable value from managing clients on the platform.

🏋️

Athletes — Free

Workout logging, AI plan generation, progress tracking, exercise library — all free. The athlete never pays. This makes it easy for trainers to onboard any client, regardless of budget.

💼

Trainers — Subscription

Client management, program delivery, workout history access, in-app messaging, plan proposals. These are the tools trainers need to run a serious coaching business — worth paying for.

PAYMENT INFRASTRUCTURE

Collecting money from Maldives.

Stripe is not directly available in MVB. Here's the stack, ranked by speed to implement.

RECOMMENDED NOW

Paddle

Acts as Merchant of Record — they charge the customer, handle all taxes and VAT globally, then pay you via bank transfer or Wise. No US entity needed. Works today.

  • ✓ 5% + $0.50 per transaction
  • ✓ Payouts to MVB via Wise
  • ✓ Handles VAT/tax globally
  • ✓ Built-in subscription management
  • ✓ No US entity required
ALSO GOOD

Lemon Squeezy

Same Merchant of Record model as Paddle. Slightly simpler setup, developer-friendly API, and strong for SaaS. Growing fast.

  • ✓ 5% + $0.50 per transaction
  • ✓ Payouts via Wise or bank
  • ✓ Built-in affiliate program
  • ✓ Great dashboard UX
  • ✓ Stripe Checkout-like embed
SCALE LATER

Stripe via Atlas

Stripe Atlas registers a US LLC for ~$500. Gives you direct Stripe access with lower fees (2.9% + $0.30) and maximum flexibility. Worth it at $2K+ MRR.

  • → $500 one-time setup cost
  • → 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
  • → Full Stripe product suite
  • → ~4–6 weeks to set up
  • → US tax filing required annually
